\section{Audit}
\label{sec:audit}

\subsection{Checks that passed}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{MuDst production re-run:} \clNSame{} of \clNSame{} production matches reproduced in the same cell,
  \clNOther{}/\clNOnlyProd{}/\clNOnlyRerun{} different/lost/new; time of flight median \clDtofMed\,ps
  (\fpath{StTofClosureMaker}, \clEvents{} events, \fpath{scripts/make_probe.C}, \fpath{logs/probe_numbers.txt}).
\item \textbf{MuDst emulation:} the emulated production choice picks the production hit for \muChoiceSame\% of the production
  matches (\fpath{scripts/make_mu.C}, \muEvMtwo{} events).
\item \textbf{Overlay bookkeeping:} matched fraction of QA tracks \ovSEMatch\% in the same event, \ovAnyMatch\% in the
  overlays without and \ovtAnyMatch\% with partner tracks; of the same-event matches \ovtSEkeep\% keep their hit,
  \ovtSEswap\% switch to a partner hit and \ovtSElose\% are lost with partner tracks (\fpath{scripts/make_ov.C}, \ovtEvMtwo{} events).
\item \textbf{Two independent MuDst estimates agree:} overlay with partner tracks \ovtFrCi\%/\ovtFrAll\% vs emulation
  \muFrProdCi\%/\muFrProdAll\% (0--5\%/0--80\%; \fpath{scripts/make_mudst_table.py}, \fpath{tables/mudst_cmp.tex}).
\item \textbf{Code identity:} md5 sums of \fpath{StTofMEQAMaker.{h,cxx}}, \fpath{runTofMEQA.C} and
  \fpath{drawTofMEQA.C} on the node equal those of the local copies (\fpath{logs/provenance.txt}, \fpath{logs/md5_local.txt}).
  The library was rebuilt (\fcmd{cons}) before the run that produced \fpath{output/qa_test_10k.root}.
\item \textbf{Event count:} \nProcEvents{} events processed out of \nChainEvents{} in the file, \nFilesRead{}
  file read (run log, \fpath{logs/runinfo.txt}); the first bin of \fcmd{hEvtCut} equals the entries of
  \fcmd{hVzAll} (\nEvtAll; \checkEvtAll).
\item \textbf{Cut-flow consistency:} entries of \fcmd{hVz} (selected events) equal the last
  applied cut-flow bin (\nEvtSel; \checkEvtSel); track counts in Table~\ref{tab:trkcut} decrease monotonically (\checkTrkFlow)
  (\fpath{scripts/make_numbers.C}, full test sample).
\item \textbf{Cell decoding:} no matched track points to a module without hits
  (\nMatchedInEmpty{} entries), consistent with the same \fcmd{btofCellId}/\fcmd{id()} encoding for traits and hits.
\item \textbf{PID scale:} fitted \mtwo{} peaks of $\pi$, K, p agree with PDG within 0.005\,GeV$^2/c^4$
  (\fpath{scripts/make_numbers.C}, $0.4<p<1.0$\,\GeVc).
\item \textbf{$z_{\rm local}$ window:} the edges at $\pm3.05$\,cm match \fcmd{mZLocalCut=3.05} in
  \fpath{StRoot/StBTofMatchMaker/StBTofMatchMaker.cxx} of SL24y (source inspection).
\item \textbf{API:} picoDst getters and compression factors were read from the SL24y
  \fpath{StRoot/StPicoEvent} headers (source inspection).
\item \textbf{Geometry fit:} mean fit residual \geoFitRmsTest\,cm over \geoNFitTest{} modules; rotated-copy closure
  \geoClosureMeanTest\,cm mean (\fpath{macros/makeGeom.C}, \fpath{logs/makeGeom_test.log}).
\item \textbf{Production neighbour rule:} the cell$\pm$1 acceptance and \fcmd{mWidthPad}=3.45\,cm were read in
  \fpath{StBTofMatchMaker.cxx} (SL24y); the fitted pitch agrees.
\item \textbf{Same input sample:} the mixed-event test selects the same \meEvtSelTest{} events as the QA. It has
  \meNTrkQATest{} QA tracks, 77 fewer than the QA maker (\nTrkEta), because the test build compared
  nHitsFit/nHitsMax with the double 0.52 instead of the float 0.52 used in the QA maker (tracks with ratio exactly
  13/25 were dropped). Fixed before the 1M-event productions.
\item \textbf{1M merge:} all \meNJobsVthree{} job logs end normally and contain the maker summary; the merged file has
  \meEvtAllVthree{} events read (\fpath{scripts/merge_v3.sh}, \fpath{logs/hadd_v3.log}); the job file lists contain no
  duplicate file (checked on the node).
\item \textbf{Path length and $m^2$:} for tracks whose production hit is chosen, $L$ agrees with $\beta_{\rm prod}c\,t_{\rm prod}$
  (mean \mtDLMeanVthree\,cm, RMS \mtDLRmsVthree\,cm) and $m^2$ with production (RMS \mtDmRmsVthree\,GeV$^2$/$c^4$)
  (\fcmd{hDLVsP}, \fcmd{hDm2VsP}, \fpath{scripts/make_m2.C}).
\item \textbf{Production hit choice:} largest ToT among candidate cells, then closest $y$, read in
  \fpath{StBTofMatchMaker.cxx} (SL24y, lines 782--833).
\item \textbf{Background diagnostics:} every v4--v8 job log ends normally; all passes read the same \meEvtAllVthree{} events
  (v6: 25\,000 per run) (\fpath{scripts/merge_v4.sh}--\fpath{merge_v8.sh}).
\item \textbf{Overlay ME does not exceed SE:} largest ME/SE over $m^2$ bins is \mtValleyAllVthree{} in 0--5\%,
  $0.4<p<1.5$\,\GeVc{}, against \mtValleyPlainMinVthree--\mtValleyPlainMaxVthree{} without overlay (\fpath{scripts/make_m2.C}).
  It reaches 1.21 at $0.2<p<0.4$\,\GeVc{} (Table~\ref{tab:m2contam}).
\item \textbf{$P_{\rm ME}$ = occupancy:} slope of $P_{\rm ME}$ vs $N_{\rm hits}$ is \meSlopeRatioTest{} times $1/23040$
  (\fpath{scripts/make_me.C}, test sample).
\item Simulation: fast TPC simulator vs full TPC response on the same \simEvGenOneK{} GEANT events: category fractions agree within
  \simTrsMaxSig\,$\sigma$ (\fpath{scripts/make_sim.C}, Table~\ref{tab:simtrs}). Every TOF-matched simulated track has a truth class
  after the \fpath{StBTofSimMaker} truth-id patch: \simNoTruth{} matches without truth in the 50k sample (\fpath{logs/sim_numbers.txt}).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Not verified (assumptions)}
\begin{itemize}
\item MuDst: the database time stamp of the re-run is not pinned to the production; the residual ps-level differences
  are attributed to later calibration updates (not checked).
\item MuDst: the restore of the event hit and track arrays after each overlay is not tested directly; the overlay matched
  fractions are consistent with it.
\item MuDst: the partner tracks are selected with $n_{\rm HitsFit}\ge15$ and $0<{\rm flag}<1000$, a superset of the match
  maker's valid tracks (the maker applies its own cuts); their effect on the event's own matches (\ovtSElose\% lost) is not
  corrected.
\item MuDst: \ovEvMtwo{} (overlay) and \muEvMtwo{} (emulation) events are used; the difference comes from the start-time
  requirement (VPD start vs own $T_0$), not checked event by event.
\item MuDst: the statement that the overlay ME follows the SE shape and that the pion-side spike disappears is read by eye
  (Fig.~\ref{fig:ovtrk_m2_cent}); \fpath{tables/m2_v9.tex} is a stored output of \fpath{scripts/make_m2.C}, not regenerated by
  \fpath{build.sh}.
\item Minimum-bias trigger IDs other than 640051 (not present in the test file).
\item That the picoDst keeps all BTOF hits of an event (only inferred from equal mean/RMS of
  hit count and \fcmd{btofTrayMultiplicity}).
\item Meaning of trigger IDs 6, 35, 36, 37 and the 6406xx/6408xx IDs.
\item Representativeness of run 20093036 for the dataset; no run-by-run QA.
\item Catalog file/event counts are copied from the catalog query output in \fpath{logs/catalog_P24iy.txt}.
\item The availability of NFS replicas was checked only for the latest runs (first 3000 catalog
  entries ordered by run number), not for the whole list.
\item The geometry is fitted and the ME test is run on the same events (test sample). For the 1M sample the
  refitted geometry agrees with it to \geoCmpMeanVthree\,cm (\fpath{scripts/make_me_runs.C}), so this is checked for the runs used.
\item The time distribution of BTOF hits without a stored time (\mtMETimedFracVthree\% of the chosen partner hits have one)
  is the same as for timed hits (all-hits normalization of Sec.~\ref{sec:m2}).
\item StRefMultCorr centrality and pile-up definitions for Run19 19.6\,GeV were used as provided (not re-derived).
\item ToT of a hit is independent of its origin (equal-chance emulation, Sec.~\ref{sec:bkg}); earliest time as a ToT proxy.
\item The growth of the BTOF hit multiplicity between 70--80\% and 0--5\% is taken from refMult, not computed per class.
\item Shape statements on the $m^2$ overlays (position of the random peak, where ME follows SE) are read by eye.
\item Mixed-event independence of random hits on the track (no correlation of noise or afterpulses with real hits).
\item Visual statements (shape of $y_{\rm local}$ tails, $\phi$ dip near $-0.6$, $\eta$ dip of the
  efficiency at $\eta\approx0$) are read from the figures, not fitted.
\item Simulation: GEANT3 hadronic model and cuts for slow secondaries in the BTOF trays; ToT$\,=0$ of the fast BTOF simulator;
  centrality from the simulated refMult percentile; shapes in Fig.~\ref{fig:sim_qm2} and the decay radius range are read by eye.
\end{itemize}
