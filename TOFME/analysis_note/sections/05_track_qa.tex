Table~\ref{tab:trkcut} gives the track yields. The quality cuts keep most primary tracks, and the
$|\eta|<1$ cut keeps \fracEtaKept\% of those: with the inner TPC (iTPC) upgrade in Run19, primary tracks
extend well beyond $|\eta|=1$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:trk_eta}). The QA sample has \perEvtTrkEta{} tracks per event,
mean $p_T$ = \meanPt\,\GeVc.
